Welcome, Fáilte

Welcome to my website where I showcase my projects. The purpose of this site is to share some information on things I've been working on, serve as a way to share knowledge, and act as a portfolio of my work.

Bio

I'm someone who has always been drawn to the magic of how things work — from electricity and mechanics to software and the wider world of science. For as long as I can remember, I've enjoyed taking things apart, putting them back together, and trying to understand the principles behind them. Whether it's building something practical, tinkering with a silly idea, or exploring a strange phenomenon, I find joy in the process of learning and making.

I believe anyone can achieve remarkable things when they put their mind to a problem. The satisfaction of solving something — whether big or small — is hard to beat. I come from rural Ireland and have spent years working on projects that range from the odd and quirky to the genuinely useful. Every project teaches me something new.

This website is a way for me to share some of those projects, not to showcase polished products, but to spread curiosity and encourage others to experiment, build, and learn. To me, science and engineering aren't just careers — they're ways of seeing the world. As Arthur C. Clarke famously said, "Any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic." The more we understand, the more magical the world becomes.
